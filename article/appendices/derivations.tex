\section{Analytic Derivations}
\label{app:derivations}

This appendix collects derivations that support the main text without
interrupting its flow. For example, an intermediate step in deriving
Eq.~\eqref{eq:action} may be written as
\begin{equation}
  \int \mathrm{d}^d x\, \partial_\mu J^\mu = 0,
\end{equation}
assuming fields vanish sufficiently rapidly at the boundary.
